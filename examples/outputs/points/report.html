Eligible injections are treated as independent Bernoulli trials. Blending, crowded photometry, and a shared detection threshold violate that assumption, so these intervals are binomial uncertainties rather than a full survey covariance. The Wilson interval is the one to quote. The percentile bootstrap resamples the observed recoveries with replacement and can collapse when every trial has the same outcome.
